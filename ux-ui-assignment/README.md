**Recommended order:** 01 first (end-to-end design process), then 02 (system thinking) or 03 (sensitive flows and content), by the role being hired. Do not ask every applicant to complete all three.

## Rules for candidates

- Complete only the assignment selected for you.
- Read [design-instructions.md](design-instructions.md). It applies to every assignment.
- Use synthetic content only. No real people, customers, products or production data.
- Personas and research are assumptions. Label them. Do not present invented research as real.
- Start from [../shared/design-system/README.md](../shared/design-system/README.md) or replace it and explain why.
- Submission window 3 calendar days, then a design discussion.
- Follow [../SUBMISSION_FORMATS.md](../SUBMISSION_FORMATS.md) where it applies.
